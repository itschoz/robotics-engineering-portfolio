# About this portfolio

This portfolio documents a practical learning journey toward robotics engineering.

## Technical focus

- **Robotics middleware:** ROS 2, DDS, QoS, topics, services, and actions
- **Simulation:** URDF, TF2, and MuJoCo
- **System integration:** ros2_control and hardware abstraction
- **Embedded systems:** STM32, UART, PWM, timers, and encoder feedback

## Engineering approach

I aim to connect simulation and physical hardware through consistent interfaces, then validate behavior with reproducible experiments and clear documentation.

Only work that has actually been implemented or tested will be described as completed. Planned work and hypotheses will be labeled accordingly.
